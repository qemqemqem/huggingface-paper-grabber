A good paper focuses on one or more of the following domains:

* Neuroscience-inspired machine learning, including spiking neural networks and biologically plausible alternatives to backpropagation
* Reasoning and cognitive architectures in AI, such as automated reasoning or cognitive backtracking
* Reinforcement learning, especially as it relates to memory, agentic behavior, or integration with LLMs
* Agentic behavior, including the nuances of tool use, scaffolding, and context engineering
* AI alignment and safety, with emphasis on interpretability

A good paper clearly articulates the underlying mechanisms and provides insight into how information is represented or processed
It draws meaningful connections to biological intelligence or cognitive science where relevant
